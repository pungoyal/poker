How it works

Heads-up No-Limit Hold'em against one of four kinds of player. Stacks reset every hand, so every hand is a fresh decision. The coach reads the opponent's likely hands from what it does and prices each of your options against them: your result swings with the cards, the chips given up to mistakes measure how you played.
